01Photos and camera

When you tap Take Photo, Hekip uses the camera to capture a stamp picture. When you tap Choose Photo, Hekip reads a picture you select. When you tap Save Image, Hekip writes the finished scrapboard into your photo library.

Pictures are stored in the app’s private folder. Hekip does not upload them.

02Microphone and speech

When you tap Speak Text, Hekip uses the microphone and on-device speech recognition to fill the scrapboard text field. Nothing is written until you confirm the transcript.

If you decline the microphone, you can still type your text.

03Purchases

Board exports spend Notes, a consumable balance stored on this device. Purchases are processed by Apple. Hekip keeps a local ledger of verified transactions so the same purchase is not granted twice.

Notes cannot be restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared.

04Account

Enter creates a session on this device only. There is no Apple ID login, email, or password.

Log Out keeps stamps, pages, and Notes. Delete Account erases local stamps, scrapboards, exported files, demo history, and remaining Notes.

05Tracking and notifications

After you enter the atelier, iOS may ask about tracking and notifications. Those are standard system alerts and you can change your choice in Settings at any time. Hekip does not sell personal information.

06Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Hekip page on the App Store. The operator is Hekip.
