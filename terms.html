01The atelier

Hekip turns photos you take or choose into perforated stamps and scrapboard pages. Exports are PNG files saved in the app.

Preview does not spend Board Credits. Confirming an export spends 1 Board Credit when the page is saved.

02Board Credits

A welcome balance is placed on this device. Additional Board Credits are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. Prices and names are shown from the App Store.

Board Credits stay on this device and are not restored after Delete Account or removal of the app.

03Your content

You keep the photos you provide. Do not use Hekip to infringe someone else’s rights.

Deleting a stamp removes the source file when no exported page still needs it. Exported pages can remain after a source is deleted.

04Log Out and Delete Account

Log Out ends the on-device session and keeps your work. Delete Account erases the local session, stamps, pages, demo history, and remaining Board Credits.

05Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the Hekip page on the App Store. The operator is Hekip.
